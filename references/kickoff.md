# Kickoff: their direction, their material, then your pitch

Don't open with a questionnaire, and don't guess the direction from the
files alone. Ask what they're picturing, look at everything they sent,
then pitch directions that start from their idea. Ask only what's still
unanswered after that.

## 1. Ask for their direction and their material
Open with one short message that asks both, something like: "What are
you picturing, even roughly? What's it for, what should it feel like,
anything you've seen that you'd want it to be like? And send me
whatever you've got: a song, voice memos, photos, videos, a logo, a
story. I'll look through it and pitch you some directions."

- If they already said what they want, don't ask again; confirm it in a
  line and move on.
- If they sent files with no direction, still ask what they have in
  mind before pitching. One open question is enough.
- If they genuinely have no idea ("you tell me"), that's fine: pitch
  from the material alone in step 3.
- If they have an idea but no files, skip step 2.

## 2. Study everything they sent
Look at every file before saying anything else.
- **Photos and images:** subjects, setting, light, colors, condition,
  anything funny or striking, what's real and recurring (a car, a pet, a
  product, a person).
- **Video:** extract frames if you can run code; otherwise ask them to
  describe it or send stills. Note what's usable as real footage.
- **Audio:** with code, measure length, tempo, and sections, and
  transcribe any words. Without code, ask for the length, genre, and
  lyrics. You can't hear mood, so ask how it should feel.
- **Text** (story, lyrics, script, notes): the premise, tone, and any
  wording that must stay.

Write a short "what I see" note in `brief.md`: each asset, one line
each, plus anything that stood out.

## 3. Pitch two or three directions
Their direction comes first. The first pitch is their idea, made
concrete with their material and done as well as possible. The others
are variations on it (a different tone, format, or angle you think
could land better), not unrelated ideas. Only when they had no direction
are all the pitches your own.

Each pitch, a few lines:
- a title and one-sentence premise built from their idea and material
- the format (profile) and why
- the look in a few words
- how timing works (to the song, to a narration, to a fixed ad length)
- the motion mix and a rough credit range from `model-notes.md` (a 2:37
  mostly-stills episode took 110 credits; a 30 s all-video music clip
  took 530 to 630)
- one line on what would need to be true (rights, consent, a voice)

Make the variations genuinely different (for example deadpan
documentary vs neon music video vs mock commercial). Say which one you'd
pick and why, without overriding what they asked for. The user can pick
one, mix them, or redirect. Iterate until they like a direction.

## 4. Fill only the gaps
Once a direction is picked, ask only what's still unknown, a couple of
questions at a time:
- **Rights** for every asset you plan to use (`rights.md`). Required.
- **Who watches it and where** (friends, a client, TikTok, a kids
  audience), if not obvious. Sets tone and content rules.
- **Length and shape**, if the pitch didn't settle it (default 9:16
  vertical).
- **Lip sync:** only if someone sings or talks on camera. Suggest ways
  to avoid it; it costs more and fails more.
- **Budget cap in credits.** Required before any generation; ask it
  with the chosen pitch's credit range in hand.
- **Approval mode:** say it will check in after every step. Mention
  autopilot only if they ask to go faster.

## Picking the profile
The pitch names it; use this to check.

| If timing is driven by | and | profile |
|---|---|---|
| a song | any | Music video |
| a narrator | anything | Narrated short |
| characters talking | a recurring made-up cast, episodes, or kids | Character cartoon |
| a fixed length | a product, service, or brand | Ad or promo |
| none of these, or a mix | | Custom |

Tie breakers:
- A song plus a product (a jingle ad): Ad, borrowing the beat-grid
  steps from Music video.
- A kids audience in any profile: also apply the kids rules in
  `profiles/character-cartoon.md`.
- A real person's face as the star: confirm consent (rule 8) before
  anything else.

## Write it down
Fill `brief.md`: the "what I see" note, the chosen pitch, audience and
purpose, timing driver, consistency targets, lip sync, format and
length, look, assets with rights status, budget cap, approval mode,
profile. Confirm it with the user before stage 1.
