# Profile: ad or promo

Evidence: untested. Built from the other three profiles plus standard ad
practice. Say so to the user, and add what you learn to "Lessons from
use".

Use for: a product, service, business, event, or brand, usually a fixed
length (6, 15, 30, or 60 s) ending in a call to action.

## Timing
The fixed length is the clock. Work backward: end card and call to
action (3 to 5 s), then the product moment, then the hook in the first
1 to 2 s. With a voiceover, the VO lines set shot lengths inside that
frame; with a jingle, use the music video beat-grid steps.

## Stages
- Kickoff adds: the offer, the call to action, the target viewer,
  brand colors and fonts, logo files, required legal lines, and where it
  runs (each platform may need its own aspect and length).
- Reference sheets for the product from real product photos. The
  product must match exactly: shape, colors, label layout.
- Voice: a VO read, or `n/a` with on-screen text.

## Defaults
- Animate the hook and the product moment; stills for supporting points
  and the end card.
- Deliver the main cut first, then other aspects or lengths as cheap
  re-edits of the same shots.

## Rules
- Real logos, product text, prices, and legal lines are composited in
  the edit from the brand's files, never generated (models garble text).
- No claims the user can't back up; no competitor marks.
- Client work: confirm the generation service's terms allow commercial
  use on the user's plan, and that the client owns every supplied asset.
