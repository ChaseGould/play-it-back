# Interview

Run this before anything else. Ask in plain language, a few questions at
a time, not as a form. Skip anything the user already told you. If the
user gave you files (a song, photos, a logo, a story), look at them
first; they answer half the questions.

## Questions
1. **What is it?** One sentence: what happens, who or what it is about.
2. **What is it for, and who watches it?** Friends, a client, a brand
   page, TikTok, YouTube, a kids audience, a pitch. This sets tone,
   content rules, and whether rights must be airtight.
3. **What drives the timing?** Pick one:
   - a song (the user supplies the audio file)
   - a narrator or voiceover telling it
   - characters talking to each other on screen
   - a fixed length (for example a 15 or 30 s ad) with text or VO
4. **Who or what must look the same in every shot?** A made-up
   character, a real person (the user or a consenting artist), a real
   product, a real car, pet, or place from the user's photos, or nothing
   recurring.
5. **Does anyone need to sing or talk on camera with matching lips?**
   Many good videos avoid it (masks, wide shots, narration). Lip sync
   costs more and fails more.
6. **Length and shape.** Default 9:16 vertical for phones; 16:9 for
   YouTube or TV. Rough length in seconds.
7. **Look.** Any reference videos, images, or words ("painted folk
   tale", "glossy neon", "claymation"). Offer two or three options if
   they have none.
8. **What do you already have?** Song, voiceover, script, story, photos,
   logo, product shots, brand colors and fonts.
9. **Budget cap in credits.** Required. Give the rough project totals
   from `model-notes.md` (a 2:37 mostly-stills episode took 110
   credits; a 30 s all-video music clip took 530 to 630) and say the
   motion mix is the main lever. Ask what they want to spend at most.
10. **Approval mode.** Default: approve after every stage. Mention that
    autopilot exists only if they ask how to go faster.

## Picking the profile
Map the answers, then say your pick and why in one or two sentences.

| If timing is driven by | and | pick |
|---|---|---|
| a song | any | Music video |
| a narrator | anything | Narrated short |
| characters talking | a recurring made-up cast, episodes, or kids | Character cartoon |
| a fixed length | a product, service, or brand | Ad or promo |
| none of these, or a mix | | Custom |

Tie breakers:
- A song plus a product (a jingle ad): Ad, and borrow the beat-grid
  steps from Music video.
- A kids audience in any profile: also apply the kids rules in
  `profiles/character-cartoon.md`.
- A real person's face as the star: confirm consent (rule 8) before
  anything else.

## Write it down
Fill `brief.md` from the answers: one-line premise, audience and
purpose, timing driver, consistency targets, lip-sync yes or no per
role, format and length, look notes, supplied assets with their rights
status, budget cap, approval mode, profile. Then confirm the brief with
the user before stage 1.
